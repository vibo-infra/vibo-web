Terms & Conditions

We wrote this to be read — not to protect us from you, but to make sure we're both clear on what VIBO is, what we promise, and what we ask of you.

Who we are

VIBO is a platform that helps people discover and attend local, in-person events. We connect attendees and hosts. We are not the organiser of any event listed on the platform — that's always the host.

What you're agreeing to

By creating an account, you confirm you're 18 or older, you'll provide accurate information, and you'll use VIBO in good faith.

Your account

You're responsible for your account. Don't share credentials, don't impersonate others, keep your contact info current. Something looks wrong? Email hello@vibo.in immediately.

Hosting events

Hosts are independent organisers. You own your event — VIBO provides the platform, not the venue, insurance, or staffing.

By hosting a paid event, you agree to our escrow payout policy: funds are held by the platform and released 48 hours after the event is confirmed complete with no active disputes.

Attending events

You agree to treat hosts and fellow attendees with respect. VIBO has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment at events.

Payments and refunds

For paid events, your payment is held in escrow until the event is confirmed complete.

If the host cancels or the event doesn't happen, you receive a full automatic refund within 5–7 business days. You don't have to ask for it.

Platform fees are non-refundable once the event has taken place.

Verification and safety

Verified hosts have confirmed their identity with a government-issued document. Documents are processed and discarded after approval — we do not store them.

Content you post

Reviews, ratings, and profile content remain yours. You grant VIBO a non-exclusive licence to display them on the platform.

What we don't guarantee

We can't guarantee every event will be exactly as described or the platform will be available 100% of the time. We do guarantee we'll act in good faith and fix problems when they arise.

How to reach us

For anything — email hello@vibo.in. We read every message.
